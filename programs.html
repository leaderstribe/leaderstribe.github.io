Program One

The Excel Program

Microsoft Excel is one of the most in-demand tools in the modern workplace — yet many women never receive formal training in it. We change that.


From managing budgets to analyzing data and building automated reports, the Excel Program teaches you everything you need to use Excel with confidence and precision.

  • Foundations: navigation, formulas, and formatting
  • Intermediate: functions (VLOOKUP, IF, SUMIF, INDEX/MATCH)
  • Data analysis: pivot tables and charts
  • Advanced: macros, dashboards, and automation
  • Real-world projects and portfolio exercises
  • Certificate of completion upon graduation

Program Two

The Leadership Program

Leadership is not a title — it's a skill set. Our Leadership Program gives women the frameworks, language, and confidence to lead teams, projects, and organizations.


Designed for women at all stages of their career, this program combines self-awareness, strategic thinking, and practical communication skills to unlock the leader within.

  • Leadership identity and personal brand
  • Effective communication and storytelling
  • Decision-making frameworks and critical thinking
  • Managing teams and navigating conflict
  • Negotiation and advocacy for yourself and others
  • Building influence without formal authority
