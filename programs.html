Program One

Excel for Data Analysis for Women

A hands-on training program that empowers women to analyze, interpret, and visualize data confidently for decision-making, business, academic work, project management, and community impact.

Beginner Level

Excel basics, data entry, formulas, formatting, charts, and everyday data storytelling. Perfect if you have little or no Excel experience.

Intermediate Level

Data cleaning, validation, lookup functions, conditional formulas, PivotTables, PivotCharts, dashboards, and insight reporting for those ready to go deeper.

Capstone Projects

  • Everyday Data Story — turning personal or work data into a clear, visual narrative
  • Community Data in Action — applying data skills to a real community or professional issue

Multiplier Model

Train-the-Trainer: Growing the Tribe

After completing Level 1 of the Excel program, participants are expected to redeliver beginner-level training to at least 10 new learners in their community or professional network — at least 50% of them women. This creates a ripple effect of digital empowerment far beyond our direct cohorts.

1. Learn

Complete Level 1 of the Excel program and build a strong, practical data foundation.

2. Teach Others

Redeliver beginner training to at least 10 new learners — at least half of them women — in your community or network.

3. Grow the Tribe

Each new learner extends the reach of Leaders Tribe, multiplying skills and confidence across communities.

The Ripple Effect

1 trained woman supports 10 new learners. With 40 women trained per year, Leaders Tribe can reach 400 additional learners through this model — growing the tribe with every cohort.

Our Teaching Style

How We Teach

Learning should feel doable, supportive, and connected to real life. Here is how we try to make that true in every session.

Practice Over Theory

You spend most of your time doing — working with real spreadsheets, real scenarios, and real exercises, not just watching slides.

Collaborative Learning

You learn alongside other women, sharing questions, wins, and ideas. No one moves through this alone.

Contextualized Content

Examples and exercises are drawn from everyday situations — work, business, school, community life — so the skills feel relevant right away.

Results-Oriented Growth

Every module builds toward something you can show — a project, a dashboard, a plan — so your progress is visible and real.
