Cost & Eligibility

Who can join, and what it costs.

Yes. Every Leaders Tribe program — Excel for Data Analysis and the Leadership Development Program — is completely free to participants. There is no tuition, no hidden fee, and no payment required at any stage of the application or training process.

No cost to apply. No cost to participate.

Our programs are designed for women aged 18 and above, including students, early-career professionals, entrepreneurs, NGO and nonprofit staff, and community advocates. You do not need any prior technology or leadership experience — our beginner tracks are built specifically for first-time learners.

You'll need a laptop or computer, a reasonably stable internet connection, and the time to attend live virtual sessions and complete practice work between classes. All programs are delivered fully online, so you can join from anywhere.

Cohorts & Applying

When the next intake starts and how to apply.

New cohort dates for both the Excel for Data Analysis and Leadership Development programs will be announced in July 2026. If you register your interest now, you'll be among the first to know once applications open and dates are confirmed.

Fill out the form on our Contact page and select "Program Application" as your reason for reaching out. Let us know which program you're interested in, and our team will follow up with next steps and confirmed cohort dates as soon as they're available.

The Excel for Data Analysis program runs in cohorts of approximately 20 women, twice a year. This keeps the learning experience personal, with enough room for real interaction, feedback, and peer support.

Program Format

How the learning experience actually works.

Excel for Data Analysis runs for 8 weeks per cohort. The Leadership Development Program runs as a cohort-based journey with live virtual sessions delivered over several weeks. Exact durations and schedules are shared with applicants once a cohort is confirmed.

Yes. Participants who complete a program receive a certificate of completion from Leaders Tribe, which you can add to your CV or LinkedIn profile.

After completing Level 1 of the Excel program, participants are expected to redeliver beginner-level training to at least 10 new learners in their community or professional network, with at least half of those learners being women. This is core to how Leaders Tribe multiplies its impact — every woman trained becomes a potential trainer herself. You can read more on our Programs page.

Our core sessions are delivered live, virtually, so you can interact directly with facilitators and fellow participants. This is paired with practice exercises and projects you complete between sessions.
