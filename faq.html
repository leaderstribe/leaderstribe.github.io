Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Leaders Tribe, our programs, and how to join.

General

About Leaders Tribe

What is Leaders Tribe? +
Leaders Tribe is a global initiative dedicated to empowering women with essential tech and leadership skills. We offer free, structured programs to help women advance their careers, build confidence, and connect with a worldwide community of like-minded leaders.
Who is Leaders Tribe for? +
Leaders Tribe is for women everywhere — regardless of age, background, education level, or prior experience. Whether you're a student, a professional looking to upskill, or someone returning to the workforce, our programs are designed for you.
Is Leaders Tribe free? +
Yes — all Leaders Tribe programs are completely free to participants. We believe cost should never be a barrier to education and empowerment. Our programs are funded through partnerships, donations, and organizational support.
Where is Leaders Tribe based? +
Leaders Tribe operates globally and online. Our participants and mentors come from over 40 countries, and all programs are delivered digitally so anyone with internet access can participate, regardless of location.


Programs

Our Programs

What programs does Leaders Tribe offer? +
We currently offer two flagship programs: the Excel Program (an 8-week tech skills course teaching Microsoft Excel from beginner to advanced) and the Leadership Program (a 10-week course developing leadership mindset, communication, and strategic skills). You can join one or both.
Do I need any prior experience to join? +
No experience is required for either program. Both the Excel Program and Leadership Program start from the ground up and are designed to be accessible to complete beginners. All you need is a device, an internet connection, and the commitment to learn.
How long are the programs? +
The Excel Program is 8 weeks long, and the Leadership Program is 10 weeks. Most participants spend 3–5 hours per week on coursework. Programs are flexible enough to work around full-time jobs, studies, or family responsibilities.
Will I receive a certificate? +
Yes! Participants who complete all required modules and projects receive a verified certificate of completion. This certificate can be shared on LinkedIn and added to your CV to demonstrate your new skills to employers.
Are the programs fully online? +
Yes, all Leaders Tribe programs are delivered entirely online. You'll access course content on our platform, attend live virtual sessions with instructors, and connect with your cohort through our online community spaces.


Community & Mentorship

Community Support

Will I have a mentor? +
Yes. Leadership Program participants are assigned a 1-on-1 mentor from our volunteer network of professional women. Excel Program participants have access to weekly group Q&A sessions and community mentors. All participants join a peer cohort and global alumni network.
Can I stay connected after graduating? +
Absolutely. Graduates become lifelong members of the Leaders Tribe alumni network — a global community where you can continue learning, connect with other women leaders, access job opportunities, and eventually mentor the next generation of participants.
Can I volunteer or mentor even if I didn't go through Leaders Tribe? +
Yes! We welcome mentors from any professional background. If you have experience in tech, leadership, business, education, or any field that can benefit our participants, we'd love to have you. Apply through our Get Involved page to become a mentor.
