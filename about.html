Who We Are

About Leaders Tribe

We build inclusive learning spaces where women gain the confidence, tools, and community to lead change — in their careers, businesses, and communities.

"Opportunity is not evenly distributed — so we work to distribute it ourselves."

Our Story

Born from a belief that every woman deserves to lead

Leaders Tribe was built on a simple observation: the barriers keeping women out of tech and leadership are not talent gaps — they are access gaps. Access to training, to mentors, to tools, and to a community that believes in you.

We create practical learning spaces where women gain confidence using digital tools, grow as leaders, and apply their skills to solve real problems — at work, in their businesses, and in the communities they care about.

Based in Lagos, Nigeria, and operating fully online, Leaders Tribe reaches women across Africa and beyond with free, structured, hands-on programs designed around how women actually learn and what their lives actually demand.

Why We Exist

Mission & Vision

Mission

"To equip women with the technological competence and leadership capacity to participate fully, confidently, and effectively in the digital economy."

Vision

"A generation of women who lead with purpose and innovate with technology, driving inclusive growth and sustainable development."

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

These principles guide every program we design, every partnership we build, and every woman we work with.

Practical Learning

We teach skills that can be applied immediately — in a job, a business, or a community project. Theory follows practice, not the other way around.

Confidence Building

Technical skill alone is not enough. We build the confidence women need to own their expertise, take up space, and lead with certainty.

Community First

No one grows alone. Our programs are built around peer learning, mentorship, and a genuine sense of belonging that extends well beyond the classroom.

Measurable Impact

We set clear targets and track real outcomes — not just completion rates, but how women are using their skills to make a difference in their work and communities.

Inclusion & Access

Cost, location, and background should never be barriers to learning. Our programs are free, online, and designed to fit the real lives of the women we serve.

Ripple Effect

Every woman we train is a potential trainer herself. We build our programs to multiply — equipping each participant to pass skills on to others in her network.

The People Behind the Work

Meet Our Team

Leaders Tribe is led by a dedicated team of educators, data professionals, and community builders who believe deeply in the power of women to lead change.

Founder & Executive Director

Nene Ibezim

Executive Director

Nene Ibezim is the Founder and Executive Director of Leaders Tribe. She is a data analyst, education development professional, and program strategist with over seven years of experience across nonprofit, higher education, and global development spaces.


Her work sits at the intersection of data, research, leadership development, and social impact. Through Leaders Tribe, Nene is building a practical learning community where women gain the confidence, tools, and support they need to grow professionally and lead with impact.


She holds an M.S.Ed. in International Education Development from the University of Pennsylvania and has worked across Nigeria, India, Malawi, and the United States. Nene is passionate about using education, data, and community to expand opportunity for women and young people.

Program Manager

Temidayo Dada

Program Manager

Temidayo Dada is an educator, education advocate, and youth development professional with over ten years of teaching experience. She holds a bachelor's degree in Animal Physiology from the Federal University of Agriculture, Abeokuta, and a Professional Diploma in Education from Corona College of Education, Lagos.


As a certified and licensed teacher, Temidayo is committed to improving access to quality education and helping young people build confidence, leadership, and a strong sense of purpose. She also serves as the Lead Volunteer of the Leading Kids Program.


A Teach For Nigeria alumna, Temidayo brings strong experience in teaching, youth empowerment, program coordination, and community engagement. She is passionate about educational equity, leadership development, and creating lasting social impact.
