About Leaders Tribe

A global movement dedicated to empowering women with the skills, confidence, and community to lead.

Our Story

Born from a Belief That Every Woman Can Lead

Leaders Tribe was founded on a simple but powerful conviction: the barriers that keep women out of tech and leadership are not talent gaps — they are opportunity gaps.


Our founders saw firsthand how women around the world — particularly in underserved communities — had the drive, intelligence, and ambition to succeed in tech and leadership roles, but lacked access to the training and networks that could get them there.


Leaders Tribe was created to close that gap. By providing free, high-quality programs focused on practical skills and leadership development, we're building a world where a woman's zip code or background does not determine her professional ceiling.

"Opportunity is not evenly distributed — so we distribute it ourselves."
What Drives Us

Our Core Values

🌍

Inclusion

We design every program with accessibility in mind. Geographic, economic, or educational barriers will not stop a woman from accessing what we offer.

💡

Empowerment

We don't just give women skills — we help them own their expertise, trust their judgment, and advocate for themselves in any room they enter.

🔗

Community

The tribe is the point. A global network of women who learn together, grow together, and open doors for each other is more powerful than any course.

🎯

Impact

We measure success by real outcomes: jobs secured, salaries raised, and women stepping into leadership roles for the first time.

🔬

Practicality

Every lesson, module, and exercise is grounded in real-world application. We teach what employers actually need and what careers actually demand.

🌱

Growth

Leadership is a lifelong journey. We support women not just through their first program, but as they advance, mentor others, and scale their impact.
