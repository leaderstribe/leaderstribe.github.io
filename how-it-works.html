How It Works

Joining Leaders Tribe is simple. Here's everything you need to know to get started.

Your Journey

From Application to Leadership

1

Apply Online

Start with our short application form. It takes about 5 minutes and asks a few questions about your background and goals. No qualifications, degrees, or experience are required — only your commitment and curiosity.

  • No prior tech or leadership experience needed
  • Open to women of all ages and backgrounds
  • Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis
5 minutes to apply.
A lifetime of impact.
2

Choose Your Program

After acceptance, you'll select your track: the Excel Program, the Leadership Program, or both. Our team will help you choose based on your goals and timeline.

  • Excel Program: 8-week tech skills track
  • Leadership Program: 10-week leadership track
  • Dual track available for committed participants
Two paths.
One destination: leadership.
3

Learn at Your Pace

Access structured modules, videos, exercises, and resources online. Attend live weekly sessions and Q&As. Work through content at a pace that fits your life — whether you're a student, working professional, or caregiver.

  • Self-paced modules available 24/7
  • Weekly live sessions with instructors and mentors
  • Real-world projects to build your portfolio
Learning that fits
around your life.
4

Graduate & Lead

Complete your program and graduate with a verified certificate, a portfolio of work, and full access to the Leaders Tribe alumni network. From here, many women go on to mentoring others — paying the opportunity forward.

  • Verified certificate recognized by employers
  • Lifetime access to the alumni community
  • Opportunities to mentor the next cohort
Graduate skilled.
Lead with confidence.
