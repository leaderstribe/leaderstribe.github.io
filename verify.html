Certificate Verification

Verify a Certificate

Enter the certificate ID printed on a Leaders Tribe certificate of completion to confirm that it is genuine.

Check a certificate ID

You'll find the ID at the top right of the certificate, for example LT-EDA-L1-2026-001-K7QX. Scanning the QR code on the certificate runs this check for you.
