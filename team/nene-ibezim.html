About Nene

Nene Ibezim is the Founder and Executive Director of Leaders Tribe. She is a data analyst, education development professional, and program strategist with over seven years of experience across nonprofit, higher education, and global development spaces.

Her work sits at the intersection of data, research, leadership development, and social impact. Through Leaders Tribe, Nene is building a practical learning community where women gain the confidence, tools, and support they need to grow professionally and lead with impact.

Background & Education

She holds an M.S.Ed. in International Education Development from the University of Pennsylvania, one of the world's leading graduate programs in international education policy and practice.

Nene has worked across Nigeria, India, Malawi, and the United States, bringing a genuinely global perspective to the design of Leaders Tribe's programs. Her cross-cultural experience informs how the organisation creates learning spaces that are inclusive, practical, and responsive to what women in different contexts actually need.

Why Leaders Tribe

Nene founded Leaders Tribe from a clear-eyed observation: the barriers keeping women out of technology and leadership are not talent gaps. They are access gaps. Access to structured training, to mentors who believe in you, to tools that are explained clearly, and to a community that holds you accountable.

She is passionate about using education, data, and community to expand opportunity for women and young people, and believes that when one woman grows, the ripple effect reaches far beyond her.
