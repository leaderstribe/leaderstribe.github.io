Bolaji Akinlabi

About Bolaji

Bolaji Akinlabi is a Program Intern at Leaders Tribe. She is a community development and social impact professional with experience in program coordination, research, content development, parenting education, and community engagement. Her work focuses on supporting initiatives that strengthen families, promote wellbeing, and create positive social outcomes.

Bolaji has a background in Adult Education, parenting and family systems, and community-centred programming — bringing a distinctive perspective to how Leaders Tribe designs and delivers its programs.

Her Work at Leaders Tribe

At Leaders Tribe, Bolaji supports the planning and implementation of programs that equip girls and young women with practical skills, confidence, and opportunities for growth. Her community development background helps the team think carefully about how to design learning experiences that meet women where they are — and support them in translating what they learn into real action in their lives and communities.

Beyond Leaders Tribe

Bolaji is also the Founder of The Guided Little Muslims, an initiative that supports Muslim mothers in raising confident, wholesome, and value-driven children. Through this work, she has developed deep expertise in parenting education, children's resource creation, and faith-based community building.

Her passion for education, family empowerment, and community development runs through everything she does — including her role at Leaders Tribe, where she is committed to building programs that create impact not just for participants, but for the families and communities around them.
