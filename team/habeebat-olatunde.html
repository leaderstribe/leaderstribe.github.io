About Habeebat

Habeebat Olatunde is the Program Coordinator at Leaders Tribe. She is a passionate advocate, educator, and communications professional with a strong interest in youth development, sustainability, and social impact.

For nearly three years, she has sensitized and educated young people in underserved communities on the Sustainable Development Goals, helping to raise young "social stewards" who can promote sustainable practices within their local communities.

Education

Habeebat is currently studying English Language at the Faculty of Arts, University of Lagos, grounding her communications expertise in rigorous academic training. Her studies complement her practical work in advocacy, youth engagement, and program delivery.

Professional Experience

Her experience as a communications specialist, strengthened through multiple internships, has equipped her with strong communication, coordination, and administrative skills. She understands how to craft clear messages, engage diverse audiences, and support the operational needs of mission-driven programs.

At Leaders Tribe, Habeebat brings these strengths to her role as Program Coordinator, where she supports program planning, participant engagement, and effective implementation, helping to ensure that every cohort runs smoothly and that participants feel supported throughout their journey.

Her Vision

Dedicated, impact-driven, and committed to personal growth, Habeebat hopes to inspire other young girls to lead with purpose and confidence. She believes that equipping young women with the right skills, networks, and encouragement can transform not just individual lives, but entire communities.
