About Temidayo

Temidayo Dada is the Program Manager at Leaders Tribe. She is an educator, education advocate, and youth development professional with over ten years of teaching experience. As a certified and licensed teacher, Temidayo is committed to improving access to quality education and helping young people build confidence, leadership, and a strong sense of purpose.

She also serves as the Lead Volunteer of the Leading Kids Program, where she supports initiatives that develop leadership skills and social responsibility among children.

Background & Education

Temidayo holds a bachelor's degree in Animal Physiology from the Federal University of Agriculture, Abeokuta, and a Professional Diploma in Education from Corona College of Education, Lagos.

As a Teach For Nigeria alumna, she brings firsthand understanding of what it means to work within under-resourced educational settings, and what it takes to inspire learning and confidence in those environments. That experience shapes how she approaches program coordination at Leaders Tribe, with empathy, structure, and a belief that every participant is capable of more than she imagines.

Her Work at Leaders Tribe

Temidayo brings strong experience in teaching, youth empowerment, program coordination, and community engagement to her role at Leaders Tribe. She is responsible for planning and running program sessions, supporting participants through their learning journeys, and ensuring that every cohort delivers on its goals.

She is passionate about educational equity, leadership development, and creating lasting social impact, particularly for women and young people who have historically had fewer opportunities to develop their full potential.
