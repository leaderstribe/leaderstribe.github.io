Our Mission

Closing the Gender Gap in Tech & Leadership

Women remain significantly underrepresented in technology and leadership roles. Leaders Tribe exists to change that—through practical skills, mentorship, and a global community of women lifting each other up.

  • Free and accessible programs for women around the world
  • Hands-on tech training aligned with real industry needs
  • Leadership development grounded in lived experience
  • A thriving global network of mentors and peers
