About Leaders Tribe

Helping women build the skills to lead change

Leaders Tribe is committed to helping women build technological and leadership skills that empower them to lead change in their communities, careers, and industries.

We create inclusive and practical learning spaces where women gain confidence using digital tools, grow as leaders, and apply their skills to solve real-world problems — at work, in their businesses, and in the communities they care about.

Practical Learning

Skills you can use immediately — in a job, a business, or a community project.

Leadership Confidence

Programs that build self-belief alongside technical skill.

Community Impact

Every woman we train becomes a source of skills for others around her.

Why We Exist

What We Focus On

Three Areas, One Goal

Everything we do is designed to help women gain real skills, real confidence, and a real community to grow with.

Technological Skills Development

We train women in high-demand digital and data skills, from digital literacy to creative and analytical tools, to enhance employability, entrepreneurship, and community impact.

Leadership Development

Our leadership programs focus on self-awareness, communication, influence, and ethical leadership, helping women lead with confidence and clarity.

Mentorship & Community Building

Through mentorship circles and peer learning networks, we foster collaboration, accountability, and shared growth among women learning to lead in technology and beyond.

Program One

Excel for Data Analysis for Women

Build confidence with data — from the basics to dashboards.

A hands-on training program that empowers women to analyze, interpret, and visualize data confidently for decision-making, business, academic work, project management, and community impact.

Beginner Level

Excel basics, data entry, formulas, formatting, charts, and everyday data storytelling.

Intermediate Level

Data cleaning, validation, lookup functions, conditional formulas, PivotTables, PivotCharts, dashboards, and insight reporting.

Live virtual classes
8 weeks per cohort
Cohort size: 20 women
2 cohorts / year (40 women)

Capstone Projects

  • Everyday Data Story — turning personal or work data into a clear narrative
  • Community Data in Action — applying data skills to a real community issue

Multiplier Model

Train-the-Trainer: Growing the Tribe

After completing Level 1 of the Excel program, participants are expected to redeliver beginner-level training to at least 10 new learners in their local community or professional network, with at least 50% of those learners being women. This helps Leaders Tribe create a ripple effect of digital empowerment.

1. Learn

Complete Level 1 of the Excel for Data Analysis program and build a strong foundation in practical data skills.

2. Teach Others

Redeliver beginner-level training to at least 10 new learners — at least half of them women — in your community or network.

3. Grow the Tribe

Each new learner extends the reach of Leaders Tribe, multiplying skills and confidence across communities.

The Ripple Effect

1 trained woman can support 10 new learners. With 40 women trained per year, Leaders Tribe can reach 400 additional learners through the Train-the-Trainer model.

Our Teaching Style

How We Teach

Learning should feel doable, supportive, and connected to real life. Here's how we try to make that true.

Practice Over Theory

You'll spend most of your time doing — working with real spreadsheets, real scenarios, and real exercises, not just watching slides.

Collaborative Learning

You learn alongside other women, sharing questions, wins, and ideas. No one moves through this alone.

Contextualized Content

Examples and exercises are drawn from everyday situations — work, business, school, and community life — so the skills feel relevant immediately.

Results-Oriented Growth

Every module moves toward something you can show — a project, a dashboard, a plan — so your progress is visible and real.
