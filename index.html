About Leaders Tribe

Helping women build the skills to lead change

Leaders Tribe creates inclusive and practical learning spaces where women gain confidence using digital tools, grow as leaders, and apply their skills to solve real-world problems.

Based in Lagos, Nigeria, and operating fully online, we support women from across the world with hands-on training, mentorship, and a community that moves together.

Practical Learning

Skills you can use immediately, in a job, business, or community project.

Leadership Confidence

Programs that build self-belief alongside technical skill.

Community Impact

Every woman we train becomes a source of skills for those around her.

What We Focus On

Three Areas, One Goal

Everything we do is designed to help women gain real skills, real confidence, and a real community to grow with.

Technological Skills Development

We train women in high-demand digital and data skills, from digital literacy to analytical tools, to enhance employability, entrepreneurship, and community impact.

Leadership Development

Our programs focus on self-awareness, communication, influence, and ethical leadership, helping women lead with confidence and clarity.

Mentorship & Community Building

Through mentorship circles and peer learning networks, we foster collaboration, accountability, and shared growth among women learning to lead.
